3 Market Context Where Egypt sources this oil, and how prices have moved historically.

§06 Where Egypt sources this oil

Context only · not a pricing input

Background market context — this does not feed into the price calculation above.

Indonesia and Malaysia are Egypt's two dominant palm oil suppliers; together they account for essentially all of Egypt's palm oil imports, with no other country coming close. Indonesia is the larger of the two.

Indonesia $811M+ import value, 2024 — Egypt's #1 supplier
Indonesia + Malaysia 224,000 t combined import volume, Jan 2026
Egypt total $1.3B total palm oil import bill, 2023
YoY change −12.7% Egypt import value, 2024 vs 2023

Malaysia's exports to Egypt dipped roughly 7.7% in H1 2025 on weak demand, then rebounded to a 13-month high in January 2026 — the Indonesia/Malaysia mix shifts with relative pricing and harvest timing rather than staying fixed.

Where this is soft: A precise Indonesia-vs-Malaysia percentage split for Egypt's imports isn't cleanly available from public web sources — Egyptian customs statistics or UN Comtrade bilateral trade data would be needed for an exact figure. The numbers above are the best directional data found: import values, combined volumes, and trend direction, not a verified market-share split.

4 Data Quality & Methodology How this model is built, and how reliable each input and forecast has been.

§09 Methodology & sources

Derivation chain

FOB olein (Bursa CPO ÷ USD/MYR, + refining/processing, olein/fractionation and physical-market-basis adjustments — see §10 for the breakdown) → origin export levy if ex-Indonesia → freight & insurance to Alexandria/Damietta → Egypt customs duty (CIF basis) → VAT if applicable → local margin & distribution → landed price in USD → converted at EGP/USD.

Where this is soft: RBD palm olein has no single public spot price for Egypt — it is a broker-quoted, opaque market. One scraped source put Malaysia FOB olein at $819/MT (24 Jul 2026), well below the ~$1,300/MT implied by CPO plus the reported refining spread and below the $1,020–1,410/MT range other sources cite globally; this model uses the spread-implied figure as the more internally consistent default and treats the $819 print as an outlier. EGP/USD forecasts for 2026 also diverge sharply by source (mid-40s to mid-50s) — the scenario band reflects that spread, not certainty. Treat every output as a starting estimate for negotiation, not a quote.

Accuracy tracking starts from the first recorded snapshot. Historical forecasts are not reconstructed. Each forecast is evaluated against the actual price on its exact target date, or the nearest logged price within the following 3 days if that date has no record — otherwise it stays Pending.
