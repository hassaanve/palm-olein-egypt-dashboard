Privacy Policy

Palm Olein Egypt project · Last updated: 31 August 2026

This is a private/internal market-information and decision-support application. It is intended for authorized project users and is not a public social or consumer service.

Information used

The application processes only information required to operate the dashboard and any explicitly approved WhatsApp notification functionality. Financial and market data used by the platform is not personal information.

Where WhatsApp notifications are explicitly authorized, a recipient phone number may be used only to address that authorized notification. The WhatsApp delivery-status service may process operational metadata such as a message ID, sent, delivered, read, or failed status, timestamps, a privacy-safe recipient reference, and provider error information when supplied.

WhatsApp messages

The current webhook implementation processes delivery-status events for operational monitoring. Incoming WhatsApp message content is not intentionally collected or stored by that implementation.

Security

Secrets, access tokens, and credentials are not intentionally exposed or stored in public pages. The project does not sell personal data.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for operational, security, audit, and troubleshooting purposes, subject to the project’s internal controls.

Requests

Authorized users may request deletion or correction of personal information through the contact method provided to them by the project owner.
